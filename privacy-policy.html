Privacy Policy for King Broast

Last updated: September 21, 2026

King Broast ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you contact us, place an order, or interact with our WhatsApp-based ordering service.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our WhatsApp ordering service, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Your delivery address and delivery area.
  • Your order details, including items, quantities, prices, and order history.
  • Your selected payment method.
  • Payment reference or transaction information that you voluntarily provide for online-payment verification.
  • Messages and information you send to our WhatsApp account that are needed to process your request or order.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process and manage food orders.
  • Calculate applicable delivery charges.
  • Contact you about your order.
  • Confirm or verify payments when necessary.
  • Provide customer support and respond to questions.
  • Maintain order and customer records for operational purposes.
  • Protect our service against misuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Our ordering service uses WhatsApp and Meta's business communication services to receive and send messages. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

4. Payment Information

King Broast may offer payment options such as Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, and Easypaisa. We do not ask customers to send passwords, PINs, or other confidential account credentials through WhatsApp. If you provide a payment reference or transaction ID for verification, we may use it only for processing and verifying the related order.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary order information with staff, delivery personnel, service providers, or technology providers when required to process and fulfill your order or operate our ordering service.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, business records, security, dispute resolution, and other legitimate operational purposes.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled through our ordering service. However, no internet or electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You may contact King Broast to ask about the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask about deletion where applicable and where we are not required to retain the information for legitimate business or legal purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Our ordering service is intended for customers who are able to place food orders and provide the required information. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
